# Epsilon-Common Knowledge

**Notation:** `C_G^ε φ`.

A temporal relaxation of common knowledge for coordination within a window of ε time units. Define E_G^ε ψ to mean that, in some ε-length interval containing the current time, each member knows ψ at some point. Then C_G^ε φ is the greatest fixed point of X = E_G^ε(φ ∧ X). It is not generally equivalent to infinitely iterating E_G^ε.
