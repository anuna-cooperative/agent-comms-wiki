# Interaction Protocols

Specifications of legal conversation patterns above the message level. Three families: ordering-based (Petri nets, state machines, AUML, FIPA interaction protocols), type-based ([[Session Types]], [[Choreographic Programming]] with [[Endpoint Projection]]), and information-based ([[BSPL]] [[Information Protocols]], where ordering and exclusion are derived from what each message requires and produces). [[Commitment-Based Protocol]]s layer meaning over any of them.

## Why protocols?

[[Knowledge and Common Knowledge in a Distributed Environment]] gives an epistemic foundation: a global task must be achieved by participants acting on local information. Protocols organise the exchanges that establish the knowledge needed for their next actions, subject to delivery and timing limits.
