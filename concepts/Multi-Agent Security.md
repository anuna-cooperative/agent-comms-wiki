# Multi-Agent Security

The discipline of securing networks of interacting AI agents against threats that *emerge from interaction* — secret collusion, swarm attacks, network-effect contagion, dispersion-based stealth. Framed by Schroeder de Witt et al. (2025; revised 2026) across both distributed and decentralised settings. Their v2 taxonomy also covers exploitation, heterogeneous attacks, attacks on overseers, mixed-motive conflict, and physical and sociotechnical threats; stealth and emergence cut across these categories. Provenance supports attribution but does not establish the truth of shared content.
