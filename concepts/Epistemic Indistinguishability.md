# Epistemic Indistinguishability

**Notation:** `(r,t) ~_i (r′,t′)`.

Two points are indistinguishable to agent i when its local view is the same at both. It cannot use that view alone to tell which point is actual. Individual knowledge requires a proposition to hold at every point indistinguishable from the current one.
