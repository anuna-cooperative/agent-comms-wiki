# Public Announcement

**Notation:** `a publicly observed information event`.

An announcement whose occurrence and content are shared under the model’s observation assumptions. In the ideal muddy children puzzle it establishes common knowledge that at least one child is muddy, even when everyone already individually knows that fact. Ordinary broadcasting does not automatically supply the reliable, mutually understood simultaneity needed for strict common knowledge.
