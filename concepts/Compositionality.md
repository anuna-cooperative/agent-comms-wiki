# Compositionality

Meaning of a complex expression is determined by meanings of its parts and how they combine — the property that lets language generalise.

## Complex-systems connection

[[Subdivided Networks]] in [[Dynamics of Complex Systems]] (§2.4, pp.342–343) provide a neural-model comparison: reusable subpatterns combine subject to compatibility constraints. This is an analogy to constrained composition, not a demonstration of compositional language semantics.
