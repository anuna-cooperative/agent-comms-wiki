# Eventual Common Knowledge

**Notation:** `C_G^◇ φ`.

A relaxation that permits members to acquire the relevant knowledge at different times without a fixed time bound. In the paper, E_G^◇ ψ means each member knows ψ at some time in the run; C_G^◇ φ is the greatest fixed point of X = E_G^◇(φ ∧ X). Reliable asynchronous broadcast can establish it. Merely promising future delivery without a delivery guarantee cannot.
