# Individual Knowledge

**Notation:** `K_i φ`.

Agent i knows φ when φ is true at every system point compatible with its local information. This is a formal guarantee about what its observations rule out, rather than a claim about confidence or an explicit internal database. Knowledge is factive: K_i φ implies φ.
