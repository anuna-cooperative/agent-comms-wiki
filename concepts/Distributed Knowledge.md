# Distributed Knowledge

A group-level epistemic operator (`D_G φ`): φ follows from pooling the information held by the members of group `G`, even if no individual knows φ. Formally, φ holds at every world compatible with all members' local views, using the intersection of their [[Kripke Structure|accessibility]] relations.

Distributed knowledge is a weaker condition on a fact than “someone knows”, “everyone knows”, or [[Common Knowledge]]: each of those implies `D_G φ`, but the reverse implications generally fail. Pooling information conceptually does not establish that a particular communication protocol can deliver it to participants. [[Knowledge and Common Knowledge in a Distributed Environment]] develops this hierarchy and connects it to coordination tasks.
