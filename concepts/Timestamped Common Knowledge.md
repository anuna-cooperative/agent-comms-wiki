# Timestamped Common Knowledge

**Notation:** `C_G^T φ`.

Common knowledge relative to the same local clock reading or protocol phase, rather than the same physical instant. Let E_G^T ψ mean every member knows ψ when its own clock reads T. Then C_G^T φ is the greatest fixed point of X = E_G^T(φ ∧ X). Identical clocks recover ordinary common knowledge at T; bounded clock skew connects it to epsilon-common knowledge. A timestamp in a message alone does not establish it.
