# Approximate Identification in the Limit

The learner eventually stabilises on a candidate whose symmetric difference with the target is finite: only finitely many elements are added or omitted. This is not a probabilistic error tolerance. In the bounded-memory paper, remembering just the preceding hypothesis suffices for every finite candidate family, although exact identification can fail.
