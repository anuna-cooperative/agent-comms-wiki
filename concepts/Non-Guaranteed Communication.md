# Non-Guaranteed Communication

**Notation:** `NG1 and NG2`.

The paper formalises unreliable delivery through two conditions. NG1 permits a continuation in which no further messages arrive. NG2 requires a processor receiving no messages in an interval to consider possible a matching execution in which no other processor receives messages in that interval. Together they support the common-knowledge impossibility theorem; this is more precise than saying the network is merely slow.
