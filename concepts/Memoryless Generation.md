# Memoryless Generation

Generation by a deterministic function of the current observed example alone, without retained examples, past outputs, or a round counter. Under finitely repeating input, every countable family of infinite languages admits such a generator with infinite-set outputs. The corresponding guarantee fails when each output must be a single previously unseen element.
