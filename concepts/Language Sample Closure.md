# Language Sample Closure

Given sample S and candidate family C, its closure is the intersection of all candidates containing S. It contains S and is a subset of the true target when the sample comes from that target. Any closure element outside S is therefore a safe novel output. For infinite families the closure may equal S; for finite families sufficiently many distinct observations force it to be infinite.
